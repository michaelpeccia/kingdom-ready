Water Calculators

The standard is 1 gallon of water per person per day, for drinking and sanitation.

How much water to store

56 gallons

Store extra in hot weather or if someone is sick. Pet water is a rough planning amount; check with your vet for your animal.

Bleach to make water safe

8 drops

For plain, unscented bleach with 5% to 9% sodium hypochlorite (CDC). Stir, then wait at least 30 minutes before drinking.

Making Water Safe: Quick Guide

From the CDC and EPA. If you don't have safe bottled water, boiling is the best way to kill germs.

1. Boil

Filter or settle cloudy water first. Bring to a rolling boil for 1 minute (3 minutes above 6,500 feet). Cool and store in clean, covered containers.

2. Bleach

8 drops per gallon of 5% to 9% unscented bleach, 16 drops if cloudy. Stir and wait 30 minutes.

3. Filter

Portable filters don't remove viruses. After filtering, add a chemical disinfectant.

Warning: boiling and bleach cannot make water safe if it contains fuel, toxic chemicals or radioactive material. Use bottled water and contact your health department.
